WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================

Phase 2 adds three pillars of intelligent timing to prevent late-entry losses:

1. AI TIMING REVIEW (SRV-302)
   - LLM validates entry timing (Groq→Gemini→fallback)
   - Confidence scoring (0–1) captures AI decision quality
   - Rejects obvious exhausted entries (e.g., "price at HOD, RSI >75")
   - Expected impact: +3–5% win rate

2. DYNAMIC THRESHOLDS (REG-201)
   - Adjusts VWAP/RSI/ATR limits per market regime
   - TREND_UP: relaxes entry (allow chasing momentum)
   - RANGE_LOW_VOL: tightens entry (be picky in choppy markets)
   - Expected impact: +2–3% win rate

3. SENTIMENT FLIP DETECTION (SEN-301)
   - Blocks equity entries for 30 min when market sentiment shifts
   - Catches BULLISH→BEARISH flips before they cause reversals
   - Allows options trading during flips
   - Expected impact: +1–2% win rate

4. BACKTEST HARNESS (BT-401)
   - Correlates entry_quality (GOOD/MID/LATE/EXHAUSTED) with PnL
   - Suggests threshold adjustments ("LATE entries lose 27% more → tighten VWAP")
   - Data-driven tuning replaces guesswork

5. TRADE ENTRY GATING (PT-402/403)
   - Checks AI approval before entering
   - Blocks entries if sentiment just flipped
   - Logs skip reason to journal for analysis

COMBINED TARGET: 62% → 70%+ win rate within 2 weeks
